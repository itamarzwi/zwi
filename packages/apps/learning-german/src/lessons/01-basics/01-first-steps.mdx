## Nouns have genders

Every German noun is **masculine**, **feminine** or **neuter**. The gender shows up in the word for "the":

| Gender    | "the" | Example      |
| --------- | ----- | ------------ |
| masculine | der   | der Mann (the man) |
| feminine  | die   | die Frau (the woman) |
| neuter    | das   | das Kind (the child) |

<Callout title="Learn the article with the noun">
The gender is usually not guessable. Always learn a noun together with its article: not "Stadt", but **die Stadt**.
</Callout>

## Nouns are capitalised

Unlike English, **all nouns** start with a capital letter, wherever they are in the sentence.

<Example de="Die Frau hat einen Hund." en="The woman has a dog." />

## Your first verbs

Verbs change their ending depending on who does the action. Here are the most important ones:

| Person        | sein (to be) | heißen (to be called) | kommen (to come) |
| ------------- | ------------ | --------------------- | ---------------- |
| ich (I)       | bin          | heiße                 | komme            |
| du (you)      | bist         | heißt                 | kommst           |
| er/sie/es     | ist          | heißt                 | kommt            |
| wir (we)      | sind         | heißen                | kommen           |
| ihr (you all) | seid         | heißt                 | kommt            |
| sie/Sie       | sind         | heißen                | kommen           |

## Numbers 1-10

eins, zwei, drei, vier, fünf, sechs, sieben, acht, neun, zehn

## Introducing yourself

<Example de="Ich heiße Anna." en="My name is Anna." />
<Example de="Mein Name ist Anna." en="My name is Anna." note="Same meaning, slightly more formal." />
<Example de="Ich komme aus Kanada." en="I come from Canada." />
<Example de="Ich bin Anna." en="I am Anna." />

## Possessive adjectives

"My", "your" and so on are placed in front of the noun.

| English | German | Example |
| ------- | ------ | ------- |
| my      | mein   | mein Name |
| your    | dein   | dein Name |
| his     | sein   | sein Name |
| her     | ihr    | ihr Name |

With feminine nouns (and plurals) an **-e** is added: **meine** Stadt, **deine** Frau.

## The past of sein and haben

sein and haben have their own past forms, war (was) and hatte (had). They are a single word, just like in English, and they work like any normal verb in second position. There is no participle at the end.

<ExampleList>
  <Example de="Ich verb(war) gestern in Marburg." en="I was in Marburg yesterday." />
  <Example de="Das Essen verb(war) gut." en="The food was good." />
  <Example de="Wir verb(hatten) keine Zeit." en="We had no time." />
</ExampleList>

This tense is called the Präteritum.

## sein: war

| German | English |
| ------ | ------- |
| ich war | I was |
| du warst | you were |
| er/sie/es war | he/she/it was |
| wir waren | we were |
| ihr wart | you (all) were |
| sie/Sie waren | they/you (formal) were |

<ExampleList>
  <Example de="Wo verb(warst) du?" en="Where were you?" />
  <Example de="Anna verb(war) letzte Woche in Litauen." en="Anna was in Lithuania last week." />
  <Example de="Der Film verb(war) sehr lang." en="The film was very long." />
</ExampleList>

<Callout title="ich and er are the same">
In the Präteritum, ich and er/sie/es have the same form, with no ending: ich war, er war. The same goes for hatte below.
</Callout>

## haben: hatte

| German | English |
| ------ | ------- |
| ich hatte | I had |
| du hattest | you had |
| er/sie/es hatte | he/she/it had |
| wir hatten | we had |
| ihr hattet | you (all) had |
| sie/Sie hatten | they/you (formal) had |

<ExampleList>
  <Example de="Ich verb(hatte) gestern keine Zeit." en="I had no time yesterday." />
  <Example de="Jess verb(hatte) einen Hund." en="Jess had a dog." />
  <Example de="verb(Hattest) du Hunger?" en="Were you hungry?" />
</ExampleList>

## Mixing with the Perfekt

When you talk about the past, use war and hatte for sein and haben, and the Perfekt for all other verbs. You will usually mix the two in the same conversation, or even in the same sentence:

<ExampleList>
  <Example de="Ich verb(war) in Marburg und verb(habe) Kaffee verb(getrunken)." en="I was in Marburg and drank coffee." />
  <Example de="Wir verb(hatten) Hunger und verb(haben) Pizza verb(gegessen)." en="We were hungry and ate pizza." />
  <Example de="Der Film verb(war) gut. Wir verb(haben) viel verb(gelacht)." en="The film was good. We laughed a lot." />
</ExampleList>

<Callout title="habe or hatte?">
In the Perfekt, haben is only a helper and stays in the present tense: "Ich habe Pizza gegessen". Use hatte when haben is the main verb, like "Ich hatte keine Zeit".
</Callout>
